A Croatian Tradition

The Koverta

Dominik comes from a Croatian family, and over there a gift almost always comes the same way. A little cash, tucked into a beautiful card, sealed in an envelope called a koverta. Bought presents and registries have never really been a thing. Giving money towards a couple as they start their life together is simply seen as the warmest, most practical thing you can do.

There is even a moment for it. Late in the night, when the cake is cut, guests line up to congratulate the couple, hand over their koverta, and grab a drink and a little favour. We loved that so much we wanted to carry a piece of it into our own celebrations. So if you would ever like to give something, a card and a few pounds in an envelope is perfect. It was never about the amount, it is the gesture and the words inside that we keep forever.
