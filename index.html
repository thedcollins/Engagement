drinks at the bar

The food is on us, we are self-catering this one with love, saving for the wedding! So Dominik & Scott will be making everything by hand. Expect a generous spread of canapés, snacks, and cakes. There is no proper sit-down dinner though, so please have a big lunch before you come and you will be golden.

Take a Selfie

Getting Here

Flying

London Stansted (STN) is the closest. London Luton (LTN), easiest hop to Corby. About 45 minutes away and a direct train. We will be in Corby from Saturday morning until we check out Sunday afternoon, then we head to ours in Thorney, Peterborough.

Flying in just for the day is totally fine by us, but if you would like to come earlier or stay longer, just let us know on your RSVP.

Where to Stay

Top Pick · Corby

Holiday Inn, Corby

Any nights, Fri → Mon

The party itself is right here at the Holiday Inn, so you can stay exactly where the celebration is. Scott’s work has negotiated a discounted rate so we can pass this on, everyone books and pays for their own room separately (we will pass those details on to anyone that needs it). Stay whichever nights suit you, anywhere from Friday to Monday. And if you are staying over, the hotel has a spa, gym and pool you are welcome to use, so make sure to go and check it out.

In Our Village

The Rose & Crown, Thorney

Come early or stay longer

Fancy being near us rather than in Corby? The Rose & Crown in our village is a lovely spot to stay, perfect if you are coming early or making more of the trip. Thorney is a pretty model village out in the fens, rebuilt by the 7th Duke of Bedford, whose Duchess, Anna, is the very person credited with inventing afternoon tea. Rooms are booked & paid by you.

Staying in Thorney? We will get you there

Thorney sits just outside Peterborough. If you are staying at the Rose & Crown, get a train to Peterborough and one of us will pick you up by car. Heading home, you would travel Peterborough → Airport, or we will try to run you to the airport ourselves. Just tell us on your RSVP and we will figure it out together.

When you RSVP, you will tell us which nights you are coming in, who is travelling with you and where you would like to stay. This way we can gather all the dates and organise everything.
