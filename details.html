A Croatian Tradition

The Koverta

Your showing up is the biggest gift we could ask for, so please do not bring a gift.

We know through experience that no matter how many times we say that, bottles of wine, chocolate and flowers inevitably turn up. We rarely drink, are allergic to dairy and flowers make Scott sneeze, so please do not waste your hard-earned cash on these small gestures, as we hate anything going to waste.

However, if you insist (as we know some people do), then we will follow a Croatian tradition — the koverta.

Dominik comes from a Croatian family, and over there a gift almost always comes the same way. A little cash, tucked into a beautiful card, sealed in an envelope called a koverta. Bought presents and registries have never really been a thing. However, a gift like this can make a big difference to a couple as they start their life together, and is simply seen as the warmest, most practical thing you can do.

Please just remember, you joining us and making this a night of the best memories is the only gift we want — this is simply a footnote to make sure there are no wasted bottles of wine and chocolates.
